Research Identity Governance

P07 is the authority and verification layer of the seven-page knowledge system. It defines the relationship between the person, research, outputs, identifiers, infrastructure, and external authority records.

Profile pages belong primarily to P05. Applied knowledge and books belong to P02 or P03. Research objects belong to P04. Controlled concepts belong to P06. P07 maintains the identity, authority, and relationship layer connecting these components.

This separation prevents the research identity page from becoming a duplicate catalogue, social wall, product page, or generic CV.

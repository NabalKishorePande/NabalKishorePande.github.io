This page will contain the identity and authority layer connecting the person, research areas, research outputs, persistent identifiers, external authority records, and research infrastructure.

Page Foundation

Research Identity & Authority establishes the relationship architecture through which research identity can be connected to research, outputs, identifiers, infrastructure, and external authority or bibliographic records.

The page will distinguish the canonical public knowledge website, research infrastructure identity, persistent identifiers, and bibliographic or scholarly authority records.

The verification structure will connect a person to research, research outputs, identifiers, and appropriate external authority records without treating unrelated platforms as interchangeable identity evidence.

Person → Research → Output → Identifier → External Authority Record

Authority Record Rule

P07 describes the relationship between the researcher and an external authority record before presenting the destination as an identity connection.

Identity Before Link

Every important external record is introduced with its role in the identity or authority system.

Relationship Before Destination

The page explains why the record is relevant before directing the reader to the external platform.

Ownership Before Duplication

Each external record is assigned to the appropriate P02, P03, P04, P05, or P07 layer.

Verification Before Authority

External records should only be treated as identity relationships when the record is genuine, attributable, and verifiable.

Research Identity Governance

P07 is the authority and verification layer of the seven-page knowledge system. It defines the relationship between the person, research, outputs, identifiers, infrastructure, bibliographic records, knowledge-graph entities, and external authority systems.

Profile pages belong primarily to P05. Applied knowledge and commercial publishing destinations belong to P02 or P03. Research objects and provenance belong to P04. Controlled concepts belong to P06. P07 maintains the identity, authority, and relationship layer connecting these components.

This separation prevents the research identity page from becoming a duplicate catalogue, social wall, product page, or generic CV.

Canonical ownership rule: P07 may reference an object owned by another page when the reference establishes an identity, authority, bibliographic, or knowledge-graph relationship. The referenced object remains owned by its designated page.

A PRACTICAL DECISION WORKFLOW

Move from Uncertainty to an Actionable Decision

Use a structured sequence to make your reasoning more transparent and your next steps easier to manage.

STAGE 1

Diagnose First

Separate symptoms from the underlying problem. Define the decision that actually needs to be made.

STAGE 2

Evaluate Evidence

Distinguish documented information from assumptions, incomplete data and untested expectations.

STAGE 3

Compare Alternatives

Use relevant criteria, constraints, trade-offs and risks to compare available options.

STAGE 4

Plan Execution

Translate the decision into tasks, responsibilities, milestones and review points.

READINESS · DECISION · EXECUTION

The RDE Approach

The Readiness–Decision–Execution approach organises practical thinking around three questions. It can be used as a planning aid in education, research, career decisions and professional work.

  • Readiness: Do I have the information, capability and resources required?
  • Decision: Which option best fits the goal, constraints and evidence?
  • Execution: What actions, milestones and reviews will move the decision forward?

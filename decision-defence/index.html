A Practical Decision Sequence

Three Questions to Ask Before Acting

Use these questions as a starting point before making a professional, organisational, research, or personal decision.

01

Have I Diagnosed the Real Problem?

Separate visible symptoms from possible causes. Define what is happening and identify which evidence could distinguish between explanations.

02

What Evidence Supports Each Option?

Separate verified facts from assumptions. Compare realistic options using explicit criteria, constraints, and relevant risks.

03

How Will I Review the Outcome?

Record the reasons for the decision, define the next action, and establish what result or new evidence would justify a review.

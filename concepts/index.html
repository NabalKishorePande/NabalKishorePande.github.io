P06 · Controlled Semantic Layer

CONCEPTS

A controlled semantic knowledge layer defining the core concepts used across Scholarship Intelligence, Decision Defence, Global Professional Mobility, research, and practical application.

Role of P06

P06 is the canonical definition and relationship layer of the seven-page knowledge system.

It defines reusable concepts once and connects them to their applied domains, research records, and canonical destinations.

P06 is not a second research page, a second profile directory, or a generic glossary.

Governance rule: Define once. Relate explicitly. Apply in context. Do not duplicate canonical definitions on domain, research, profile, or authority pages.

Decision Defence

An approach to making important decisions explainable, evidence-based, traceable, and accountable.
Scope
Evidence, rationale, criteria, traceability, accountability, and decision documentation.
Related concepts
Decision Defence Architecture, Decision Evidence, Decision Rationale, Decision Traceability, Decision Accountability.

Decision Defence Architecture

A formal architecture for organising evidence, criteria, rationale, decision records, traceability, and accountability around important decisions.
Scope
Decision evidence, criteria, rationale, decision records, review, traceability, and accountability.
Related concepts
Decision Defence, Decision Evidence, Decision Rationale, Decision Traceability, Decision Accountability.

Scope
Professional movement, portability, credential recognition, and access to international professional opportunities.

Concept Governance

A concept should be added when it represents a meaningful and reusable semantic object in the knowledge system. Concepts should not be added only to increase page length or search-term coverage.

Where a concept is used across several pages, its controlled definition belongs here while its applied context remains on the appropriate domain page.

Canonical ownership rule: P06 owns the definition and semantic relationship. P02/P03 own application. P04 owns research evidence and provenance. P05 owns verified external research profiles. P07 owns research identity and authority relationships.

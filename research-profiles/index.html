Research Profile vs Research Output

A research profile identifies the researcher on an external scholarly system. A research output is the work produced by the researcher.

P05 · Research Profile

Identifies the researcher in an external scholarly environment.

→
P04 · Research Output

Records the research paper, dataset, report, framework, or other research object.

Anti-duplication rule: P05 does not reproduce the complete research record of a paper, dataset, report, or framework. The canonical research object remains on P04 · Research.

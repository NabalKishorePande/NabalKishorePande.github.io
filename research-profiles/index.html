Publishing Profile Ownership

External Record Principle

Each external record should have a legitimate relationship with the knowledge system.

An external profile may provide identity, research discovery, infrastructure, publishing, distribution, transaction, or authority value. The link should therefore explain what the destination represents rather than function as an arbitrary collection of social links.

Relationship rule: Explain first. Connect second. Verify where necessary.

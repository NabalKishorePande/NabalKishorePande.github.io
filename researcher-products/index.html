Buyer Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about research tools, book identifiers, digital formats, academic English resources and product availability.

Which research tools are available for thesis and proposal planning?

The catalogue includes a free Research Alignment Checklist, a Research Alignment Diagnostic System, a Research Proposal Template, a Response to Reviewers kit and a systematic review workbook. Each serves a different stage of research planning or revision.

Can these research workbooks guarantee thesis approval or journal publication?

No. They are practical self-audit and planning aids. They do not replace supervisor feedback, discipline-specific methods advice, ethical review or journal requirements, and they do not guarantee approval, funding or publication.

Which books by Nabal Kishore Pande have ISBNs?

The publication catalogue below lists six ISBN-identified non-fiction titles: Institutional English, The Academic Word Engine, RDE Field Manual, The Readiness–Decision–Execution Model, Cognitive Test Readiness Framework and Interview Failure Due to English.

What is the difference between an ISBN book and a digital product?

An ISBN identifies a specific book edition. Digital products such as spreadsheets, templates, checklists and PDF toolkits are listed separately by platform and product format. Some ebooks also have ISBNs, so print versus digital is not the same distinction as ISBN versus non-ISBN.

Which resource helps with academic English and CEFR B2-to-C1 development?

The Academic Word Engine focuses on academic vocabulary, semantic families, collocations, register, cohesion, hedging and controlled use in writing and speaking. Cognitive Test Readiness Framework addresses independent preparation and transferable skills relevant to ACT, IELTS, TOEFL, PTE Academic, Duolingo English Test and EF SET. Neither guarantees a particular score or proficiency level.

Which book focuses on interview communication for international job seekers?

Interview Failure Due to English: A Performance-English Workbook for Global Job Seekers from India, Africa & Southeast Asia focuses on diagnosing unclear interview answers, organising evidence, practising difficult questions and improving clarity under pressure.

Where can I find international career and scholarship planning resources?

The digital products section includes the Global Career Portability System and the Study Abroad Scholarship Application Guide. They help users organise research, evidence, deadlines and planning decisions. Always verify current immigration, eligibility, funding and qualification rules with official sources.

Are the English preparation books affiliated with IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, DET, OET or EF SET?

These are independent educational publications. Their references to named examinations describe subject coverage, not provider endorsement. Consult each examination provider's official website for current test formats, registration rules and score acceptance policies.

Where can I check current prices, formats and availability?

Open the relevant Amazon, Booktopia, Leanpub, Payhip or Gumroad listing. Retailers and creator platforms control their prices, product availability, file formats and delivery options; these details can change.

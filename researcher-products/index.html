Frequently Asked Questions About Non-Fiction Books and Research Products

Answers to common questions about ISBN publications, academic English, decision-making books, research workbooks and digital product formats.

What is the listed price of Institutional English by Nabal Kishore Pande?

How does The Academic Word Engine help with the transition from CEFR B2 toward C1?

The book describes a structured learning architecture built around academic vocabulary, semantic families, collocations, academic register, cohesion, hedging and controlled use in writing and speaking. Its catalogue description also outlines a review loop and a 90-day activation schedule. Progress depends on the learner's starting level, practice and feedback; no particular exam score or CEFR outcome is guaranteed.

What is the difference between an ISBN publication and a digital workbook?

An ISBN identifies a particular book edition. Digital workbooks and creator-platform resources are practical products whose formats and identifiers depend on the individual item. Some ebooks also have ISBNs, so the distinction is not simply print versus digital. This page identifies ISBNs where they are documented and lists practical digital products separately by platform.

Which book covers decision-making and opportunity evaluation?

RDE Field Manual and The Readiness–Decision–Execution Model focus on readiness, opportunity fit, evidence, risk, decision records and execution planning. The Leanpub catalogue also includes DECIDE WELL, Choosing Strategic Priorities, DIAGNOSE FIRST and The Decision That Changed Nothing.

Which resource helps researchers check a thesis or research proposal?

The free Research Alignment Checklist provides 25 yes/no checks. The Research Alignment Diagnostic System adds an Excel-based self-audit with mismatch flags and a severity report. The Research Proposal Template provides prompts, a rubric and a worked example. These resources support review but do not guarantee approval, funding or publication.

Where can I find scholarship and international career planning resources?

The Payhip catalogue includes the Global Career Portability System and the Study Abroad Scholarship Application Guide. These resources help organise planning tasks, evidence, deadlines and decisions. Always verify immigration, eligibility, funding and qualification requirements with the relevant official sources.

Are the books and workbooks affiliated with IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, DET, OET or EF SET?

The titles address preparation or skills related to the named examinations. They are independent educational publications and should not be understood as affiliated with or endorsed by the examination providers unless an official provider explicitly states otherwise. Check each provider's official website for current test formats, registration requirements and acceptance policies.

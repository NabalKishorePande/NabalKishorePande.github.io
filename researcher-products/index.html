Why Use Structured Research Resources?

These resources are designed to make research tasks more organised and reviewable. They support your workflow; they do not replace disciplinary expertise, supervisor guidance, ethical review or independent judgement.

1

Plan the research

Clarify the question, objectives, methods, data and analysis before committing to the full study.

2

Record the work

Use templates and trackers to document decisions, comments, revisions, evidence and outstanding tasks.

3

Review before submission

Run structured checks, identify weaknesses and decide what needs attention before the next stage.

Research Orientation

The knowledge system is organised around three connected areas: Scholarship Intelligence, Decision Defence, and Global Professional Mobility.

The common orientation is structured knowledge: identify the relevant problem, organise evidence, define the concepts involved, connect the evidence to a decision or application, and preserve the relationship between the knowledge object and its source or record.

Research provides the evidence layer. Concepts provide controlled meaning. Applied domain pages connect knowledge to practical decisions and professional use. Research identity and authority provide the verification layer.

The Knowledge System

The website is one connected knowledge system rather than seven independent websites. Each page provides a distinct view of the same underlying relationships.

Core relationship: Identity establishes the person and orientation; domain pages organise applied knowledge; research establishes evidence and provenance; concepts establish controlled meaning; authority establishes verification; application connects knowledge to use; and transaction provides an appropriate commercial destination where one exists.

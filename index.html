What is Scholarship Preparation Intelligence?

A preparation approach that treats international scholarship applications as a decision problem involving eligibility, readiness, evidence, fit, opportunity selection and execution.

How is this different from scholarship hunting?

The focus is not on collecting large numbers of opportunities. The focus is on deciding which opportunities deserve serious preparation and whether the applicant is ready to pursue them.

What does the broader research work focus on?

Decision Systems, Research Frameworks, Execution Intelligence and Scholarship Preparation Intelligence, with related work across research infrastructure, professional portability and evidence-based publishing.

Where can the research record be verified?

Persistent research records are maintained through research infrastructure including Zenodo and OSF, alongside identity and bibliographic systems serving different verification functions.

Are scholarship outcomes guaranteed?

No. The resources provide evidence, preparation systems and decision guidance. They do not guarantee admission, funding or scholarship outcomes.

Can the published work be independently verified?

Where bibliographic or persistent records exist, identifiers such as ISBN, OCLC and DOI provide external verification routes.

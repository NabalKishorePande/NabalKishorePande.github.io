Research Approach

The research layer organises questions, methods, evidence, outputs, and provenance so that research objects can be understood in relation to the work that produced them.

Research is organised through structured evidence collection, comparative analysis, structured review, dataset development, framework development, research execution, and evidence organisation.

The objective of this layer is to distinguish research claims and outputs from the applied knowledge and identity layers of the seven-page system.

Research Methods

The research system uses several complementary methods and production activities depending on the research question and evidence requirements.

  • Evidence collection — identifying and organising relevant evidence.
  • Comparative analysis — examining similarities, differences, and relationships across evidence.
  • Structured review — organising existing research through a defined review process.
  • Dataset development — converting relevant evidence into structured research data.
  • Framework development — developing structured models from research findings and relationships.
  • Research execution — managing the sequence from research question through output.
  • Evidence organisation — maintaining relationships between evidence, claims, outputs, and identifiers.

Research Evidence & Provenance

Research provenance establishes the relationship between the research question, method, evidence, and resulting output.

Research Project → Research Question → Method → Evidence → Output → Publication / Dataset / Report → Identifier
Research object record

Each research object is intended to have a defined title, type, status, research question or purpose, method, evidence, domain, related concepts, related outputs, identifier, and primary external record where applicable.

This provenance structure helps distinguish the research record from the external identity infrastructure described in Research Profiles and the authority relationships described in Research Identity & Authority.

Research Object Relationships

Research objects should be connected through explicit relationships rather than presented as an isolated catalogue.

Research Project → Research Question

A project establishes the context in which a research question is examined.

Research Question → Method

The research question informs the method used to investigate it.

Method → Evidence

The method establishes how relevant evidence is collected or analysed.

Evidence → Output

Evidence contributes to a research output such as a paper, dataset, report, or framework.
